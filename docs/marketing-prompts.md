# Club POS — Marketing Banner Prompts (ChatGPT)

Use these prompts with ChatGPT (or similar) to generate viral in-store banner **art and layout**.  
**Always add your real QR code in Canva** — AI-generated QR codes usually do not scan.

**Sign-up URL for all QR codes:** https://www.getclubpos.com

**Brand colors:** Black background, gold accent `#FFB800`, white text.

**Offer copy (current site):**
- Club POS
- $14.99/month
- 7 redemptions per month
- Members only
- Cancel anytime

---

## Workflow

1. Paste a **Main prompt** below into ChatGPT → get layout + image-generation prompt.
2. Generate the poster image (leave the **QR placeholder** empty).
3. Create a real QR at [qr-code-generator.com](https://www.qr-code-generator.com) or **Canva** → link: `https://www.getclubpos.com`
4. Drop the QR into the placeholder in Canva.
5. Export: print PDF at **300 DPI**; social crops as needed (see Follow-up prompt).

```

Create a PRINT-READY promotional banner design for a pizza shop (or neighborhood food shop) membership program called **Club POS**.

**Goal:** A catchy, viral, high-contrast poster that stops people in their tracks and makes them scan a QR code to sign up online.

**Sign-up URL (QR must link here):** https://www.getclubpos.com

**Offer details to feature clearly:**
- Brand: Club POS
- Price: $14.99/month
- Benefits: 7 redemptions per month • Members only • Cancel anytime
- CTA: "Join the Club" / "Scan to join"

**Design requirements:**
- Size: 24" × 36" vertical poster (also suggest a 11" × 17" counter version)
- Style: Bold, modern, street-food / NYC bodega energy — not corporate, not clipart
- Colors: Black background, gold/amber accent (#FFB800), white text — match a premium "members club" vibe
- Typography: Huge headline (3–5 words max), scannable from 10 feet away
- Layout: Clear visual hierarchy — headline → 3 bullet benefits → price → large QR zone → small URL text under QR

**Headline options (pick the strongest ONE and design around it):**
1. "JOIN THE CLUB. GET SLICED."
2. "$14.99/MO. 7 DEALS. YOUR SHOP."
3. "MEMBERS EAT BETTER."
4. "STOP PAYING FULL PRICE. JOIN THE CLUB."

**Subcopy (short, punchy):**
"Scan → Join in 60 seconds → Redeem in-store"

**Deliverables in your response:**
1. Final chosen headline + subcopy
2. Full layout description (where each element sits)
3. A detailed image-generation prompt I can paste into DALL·E / ChatGPT Images (specify: poster, no fake QR, placeholder box only)
4. Alt text for social posting
5. 3 hashtag sets for Instagram/TikTok in-store promo

**Tone:** Confident, fun, slightly rebellious ("insider club"), shareable on social — the kind of poster someone photographs and posts.
```
